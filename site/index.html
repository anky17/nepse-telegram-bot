TERMS OF USE

GHANTAGHAR is a free, personal, non-commercial project. There's no login, no account, and no fee to use it.

Market data is pulled from open, community-maintained archives (ShareSansarScraper and Nepse-All-Scraper on GitHub) — it's provided "as is," without any guarantee of accuracy, completeness, or timeliness. End-of-day figures aren't published until after market close, and upstream sources can occasionally be delayed or unavailable.

Nothing on this site is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Do your own research, or consult a licensed advisor, before making financial decisions.

Not affiliated with NEPSE, ShareSansar, or any brokerage. Use of this site is at your own discretion.

PRIVACY POLICY

This site has no backend server, no user accounts, and collects nothing about you. It runs entirely in your browser.

The only thing it stores is your watchlist — the symbols you star — saved in your browser's localStorage. That never leaves your device; it isn't sent to me or anyone else.

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads.

Your browser fetches market data directly from third-party open archives on GitHub — those requests go straight from your browser to GitHub's servers, not through us.
